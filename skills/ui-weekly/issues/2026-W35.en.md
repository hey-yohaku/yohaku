## Intro

Components used to ship finished. Now the useful ones ship unfinished on purpose, and let you add the opinion.

## Resources

**[shadcn/ui](https://ui.shadcn.com/)** — components you copy into your repo instead of installing. The genius move was giving up on versioning; your code, your diffs. `#components #react #tailwind`

**[Radix UI](https://www.radix-ui.com/)** — headless primitives that handle the hard 20%: focus, keyboard, ARIA. The accessibility is the product. `#components #react #a11y`

**[Headless UI](https://headlessui.com/)** — Tailwind's own take on unstyled primitives. Smaller than the competition, which is the point. `#components #tailwind #react`

## Worth reading

**[Refactoring UI](https://www.refactoringui.com/)** — the design book that treats spacing and hierarchy as a craft, not a mystery. Still the fastest shortcut from "looks broken" to "looks intentional". `#design #books #typography`

## Sign-off

The opinionless layer is winning because opinions are cheap to add and expensive to remove.
