## Intro

Most interfaces move to grab attention. The ones worth copying move to mean something. Four tools that get the difference.

## Resources

**[Motion](https://motion.dev/)** — Framer Motion stripped to its spring core and shipped framework-free. The sequencing API is the part people wanted; the React wrapper was the tax. `#motion #react #animation`

**[GSAP](https://gsap.com/)** — the timeline library that keeps outliving its obituaries. Every year brings a new animation framework, and every year they borrow its sequencing model. `#motion #animation #javascript`
![GSAP](assets/2026-W34/gsap.webp)

**[Lenis](https://lenis.darkroom.engineering/)** — smooth scrolling as a small, polite dependency. It doesn't hijack the scrollbar or fight the browser; it just rounds the edges. `#motion #scroll #javascript`
![Lenis](assets/2026-W34/lenis.webp)

## Tools

**[Framer](https://www.framer.com/)** — a design tool that grew a site builder on top of itself. The people who stay are the ones who treat its animation panel as the whole point. `#tools #prototyping #design`

## Sign-off

Motion reads as polish only when it's pointless. When it points at something, it's information.
